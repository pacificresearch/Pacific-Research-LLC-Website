# Local partner teaming agreement — term sheet

> Terms that differ from a domestic teaming agreement. Use alongside
> `capture/templates/06_subcontractor_tracker.md`. **Counsel reviews
> before signature in any new jurisdiction.**

## Parties and structure
- PRG primes; the Partner performs the defined scope as a subcontractor.
- **PRG owns the customer relationship, contract management, QC,
  invoicing, and compliance.** State this — it is what makes the
  structure legitimate rather than a pass-through, and it is what the
  ostensible-subcontractor rule looks for.
- The Partner is an independent contractor. Neither party is the other's
  agent, and neither may bind the other.
- Exclusivity for this pursuit: Y / N, and for how long.

## Scope, deliverables, and acceptance
- Precise scope, traced to the tender's own language.
- Deliverables, dates, and **PRG's acceptance right before the buyer
  sees anything.**
- PRG's QC and on-site oversight rights, including the founder's site
  visits.

## Price and payment
- **Currency of the subcontract**, and whether it matches the prime
  contract's currency. If it does not, name who carries the FX risk.
- **Pay-when-paid** tied to the buyer's actual payment, with an outside
  date. Say plainly what happens if the buyer pays late.
- Payment **by bank transfer to the Partner's own account, in the
  Partner's own name, in the stated jurisdiction. No cash, no third-
  party payees, no other jurisdiction.**
- Who pays wire and correspondent-bank fees.
- **Withholding tax** — whether the buyer or PRG withholds, at what
  rate, and whose price absorbs it. Do not leave this implied.

## Compliance flow-downs — all mandatory
- **Anti-bribery / FCPA clause**, with the signed rep letter
  (`fcpa_rep_letter.md`) attached as an exhibit.
- **Audit rights** over the Partner's books for this work.
- Sanctions and export-control compliance, with immediate notice of any
  listing.
- Every prime-contract clause the compliance matrix marks as flow-down.
- Data protection / confidentiality, and any buyer-specific data rules.
- **UN Supplier Code of Conduct** where a UN agency is the buyer.
- **PSEA** (protection from sexual exploitation and abuse) and
  safeguarding obligations where the work touches beneficiaries — UN and
  donor buyers require this and it is not optional.

## Labor and duty of care
- The Partner employs its own personnel and is responsible for
  compliance with local labor law, classification, social contributions,
  and severance. **Indemnity to PRG for misclassification claims.**
- Insurance the Partner carries, with certificates.
- **Duty of care in high-threat posts**: security, movement rules,
  hours, medevac, and next-of-kin procedure for the Partner's personnel.
  Name who provides what.

## Personnel
- Key personnel named in the proposal may not be substituted without
  PRG's written consent.
- Non-solicitation both ways, for a stated period.
- PRG's right to require removal of any individual, with cause stated.

## Term, termination, and IP
- Term tied to the prime contract; termination for convenience and for
  cause, including **immediate termination for anti-bribery breach**.
- Work product ownership — PRG owns deliverables to the extent the prime
  contract requires; the Partner keeps its pre-existing IP.
- Survival: confidentiality, anti-bribery, audit, indemnity.

## Governing law and disputes
- **Governing law and forum, stated explicitly.** A local-law, local-
  forum clause may be unavoidable and may be unenforceable in practice —
  know which before signing.
- Consider arbitration under a recognized institution with a neutral
  seat. Enforceability of a U.S. judgment in the Partner's country is
  often the real constraint; check it before relying on the clause.
