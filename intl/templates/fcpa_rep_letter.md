# Anti-bribery representation and undertaking

> Signed by every agent, partner, expert, and local hire **before the
> first payment**. Counter-signed by PRG. Filed to
> `<opportunity>/05_sourcing/vetting/`. Translate into the local
> language where that is what gets it read and understood — an
> unread undertaking protects no one.
>
> **Template only — have counsel review before first use in a new
> jurisdiction, and confirm it satisfies local anti-bribery law as well
> as the FCPA.**

---

**Between:** Pacific Research Group LLC ("PRG"), a United States
limited liability company
**And:** [PARTY LEGAL NAME] ("the Party")
**Relating to:** [pursuit / contract / country]

The Party acknowledges that PRG is a United States company subject to
the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 78dd-1 et seq.)
and that PRG may be held responsible for the conduct of those acting on
its behalf. The Party represents, warrants, and undertakes:

1. **No improper payments.** The Party has not and will not offer,
   promise, pay, authorize, or accept anything of value — money, gifts,
   entertainment, favors, employment, or any other benefit — to or from
   any government official, political party, party official, candidate,
   employee of a state-owned or state-controlled enterprise, public
   international organization official, or any other person, in order
   to obtain or retain business or secure any improper advantage in
   connection with this work.

2. **No facilitation payments.** The Party will make no facilitating or
   "grease" payment of any kind, however small, however customary, and
   however characterized locally, in connection with this work.
   **PRG's policy permits no exception.** If any official requests such
   a payment, the Party will refuse and notify PRG immediately.

3. **Disclosure of official connections.** The Party has disclosed to
   PRG in writing every owner, principal, employee, and immediate family
   member of any of them who is a government official, political-party
   official, candidate, or employee of a state-owned enterprise, and
   will disclose any such connection arising later, promptly and in
   writing.

4. **Compliance with law.** The Party will comply with all applicable
   anti-bribery, anti-corruption, anti-money-laundering, sanctions,
   export-control, tax, and labor laws of its own country, of the
   country of performance, and of the United States.

5. **Sanctions.** The Party is not, and no owner or principal is,
   listed on any U.S., UN, EU, or UK sanctions list, nor owned or
   controlled by any such person, and will notify PRG immediately if
   that changes.

6. **Payments.** The Party will accept payment only by bank transfer to
   the account identified in the underlying agreement, held in the
   Party's own name in the jurisdiction stated. The Party will not
   request or accept cash, payment to a third party, or payment to any
   other jurisdiction.

7. **Books and records.** The Party will keep complete and accurate
   books and records of all amounts received and all expenses charged
   in connection with this work, and will retain them for [X] years.

8. **Audit rights.** PRG or its representatives may audit those records
   on reasonable notice. The Party will cooperate.

9. **Subcontractors.** The Party will impose obligations equivalent to
   this letter on anyone it engages for this work, and remains
   responsible for their conduct.

10. **Notification.** The Party will notify PRG immediately in writing
    of any actual, suspected, or requested breach of this letter,
    including any request or demand from any person for an improper
    payment.

11. **Termination.** Breach of this letter entitles PRG to terminate
    the underlying agreement immediately for cause, to withhold any
    unpaid amounts, and to recover amounts already paid in connection
    with the breach. The Party will indemnify PRG against loss, penalty,
    and cost arising from its breach.

12. **Survival.** These obligations survive completion or termination
    of the work.
