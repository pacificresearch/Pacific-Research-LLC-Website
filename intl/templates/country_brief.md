# Country brief — [COUNTRY]

Built once per country, reused and updated by every pursuit there.
Live in `intl/countries/<country>.md`. **The second pursuit in a country
should be dramatically cheaper than the first — this file is why.**

## 1. Snapshot
Capital / posts of interest · languages · currency and convertibility ·
U.S. mission and its procurement office · time zone vs. ET

## 2. Sanctions and legal posture (Rail 1)
OFAC program(s) and scope · last checked (date) · general licenses
relevant to PRG's scope · UN/EU/UK listings · Transparency International
CPI score

## 3. Entry and travel (Rail 7)
Visa category for paid work · lead time · cost · passport validity rule ·
vaccinations · country clearance / eCC required · invitation letter
needed · realistic flight routing and cost from the U.S.

## 4. Security (RISK block inputs)
State Dept advisory level and date · OSAC posture · what drives the
rating · practical movement rules · medevac options and nearest adequate
medical facility

## 5. Labor and hiring (Rail 5)
Contractor vs. employee test · mandatory contract form · minimum wage ·
13th-month or statutory bonuses · employer social contributions ·
notice and severance · working-time limits · misclassification penalties ·
**available EOR providers and their fees** · typical rates by discipline

## 6. Tax (Rail 9)
Permanent-establishment threshold · U.S. tax treaty (Y/N, PE article) ·
**withholding tax on payments to foreign contractors — rate** ·
VAT/GST and whether UN/diplomatic buyers are exempt · local filing
obligations triggered by presence

## 7. Money (Rail 6)
Currency · convertibility and capital controls · **can funds be
repatriated** · banking practicalities · typical payment lag by buyer
type · wire routing and fees

## 8. Market
Local firms PRG has vetted (link to `intl/experts/`) · local firms
rejected and why · prior buyers · competitors seen · implementing
partners active in-country

## 9. PRG's honest position here
What PRG can truthfully say about its connection to this country. If
the answer is "nothing yet," write that — and it stays written until it
changes.

## 10. Sources and dates
Every claim above with where it came from and when. Facts here expire.
