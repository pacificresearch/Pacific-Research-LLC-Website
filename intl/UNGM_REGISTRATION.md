## Step 1 — Create the account (Basic registration)

1. Go to **https://www.ungm.org/** → **Sign Up** / **Register as a
   supplier** (top right).
2. Choose the **company/organization** path (not individual consultant).
   Andrew can *separately* create an individual-consultant profile later
   for roster work — keep them distinct.
3. Enter contact and account details; use
   **Andrew@pacificresearchllc.com**, not a personal address. Confirm the
   verification email.
4. Complete the Basic registration sections:
   - **Supplier information** — legal name, registration/license number,
     address, contact details, website.
   - **Countries of business** — where PRG can deliver.
   - **UNSPSC codes** — the classification that drives every tender
     notification PRG receives. **Spend real time here; this is the
     single highest-value screen in the whole process.** Suggested
     starting set, refine live:
     - 80 — Management and Business Professionals and Administrative
       Services (consulting, program management, project management)
     - 85 — Healthcare Services (clinical trial services, health
       program support, medical equipment maintenance)
     - 86 — Education and Training Services
     - 93 — Politics and Civic Affairs Services (humanitarian aid,
       community development, refugee support)
     - 70/72/76/78 — as relevant to facilities, logistics, and
       maintenance scopes PRG would aggregate
   - **UN Supplier Code of Conduct** — read and acknowledge.
   - **Declaration of eligibility** — attest PRG is not bankrupt, not
     under UN sanction, not excluded. Answer truthfully.
5. Submit. Basic registration reaches business up to **US $40,000**.

## Step 2 — Level 1 (US $40,000 – $500,000)

Requires Basic complete, plus:
- **Certificate of incorporation** or equivalent proving legal status,
  **in English** (PRG's is already in English — upload directly).
- **Three independent, non-affiliated references** with contact details.
  Independent means not owned by, employed by, or related to PRG.
  Candidates: prior institutional clients, a research sponsor, a
  university or hospital contracting office, a prime PRG has subbed to.
  **Ask permission before listing anyone.**
- **Names of owners and principals** — Andrew, sole member.

**This is the level PRG should reach.** It covers the entire realistic
UN opportunity range for a firm this age.

## Step 3 — Level 2 — NOT AVAILABLE TO PRG YET

Above US $500,000. Requires Basic + Level 1, **and the company must have
been established a minimum of three years**, plus three client reference
letters and financial documents.

PRG cannot meet the three-year test. **Any UN tender above US $500,000
is therefore a PASS on eligibility** (`intl/GATE.md` kill #11) — not a
close call, and not worth proposal hours. Record PRG's Level 2
eligibility date (three years from LLC formation) in `intl/PIPELINE.md`
and revisit then.

## Step 4 — Turn on the notifications (the actual point of registering)

In the UNGM account, subscribe to **tender notice alerts** for the
selected UNSPSC codes and countries. This is what makes UNGM a *source*
rather than a directory. Set the alerts to email
Andrew@pacificresearchllc.com so they land in the same inbox the capture
system already watches.

## Step 5 — Register the individual-consultant profile too

Separately from the company: UN agencies (WHO especially) run **expert
rosters** for individuals. The ACRP-PM/CP credentials, the MA in
International Studies, and the OAW operational record are directly
roster-relevant. Register at https://www.who.int/careers and on UNGM's
individual-consultant path. Roster placement produces work with no
proposal at all — the highest-margin outcome available in this lane.
