1.0 DESCRIPTION 

The U.S. Embassy in Canberra requires a qualified service provider to design and deliver three 
interactive U.S. studies conferences in Canberra, Sydney, and Melbourne. The project will provide 
Australian  government  officials  with  a  practical  understanding  of  the  historical,  constitutional, 
political, strategic, economic, and foreign policy foundations of the United States. 

The  program  will  use  expert-led  presentations,  facilitated  discussions,  virtual  and  in-person 
engagement,  networking  receptions,  and  pre-  and  post-program  assessments  to  strengthen 
participants’  understanding  of  U.S.  institutions,  policymaking  processes,  current  Washington 
policy settings, U.S. engagement in the Indo-Pacific, and the future of Australia–U.S. relations. 

1.1.    TYPE OF CONTRACT 

This  is  a  firm-fixed-price  contract.  The  Contractor  shall  furnish  all  labor,  personnel,  materials, 
venues, equipment, travel, logistics, and services necessary to design, coordinate, and deliver the 
U.S. Studies conferences in Canberra, Sydney, and Melbourne, including program development, 
moderators and subject-matter experts, participant assessments, networking receptions, outcomes 
reporting,  analysis,  reporting,  and  strategic  communications  support  for  the  U.S.  Embassy 
Canberra, Australia, as described in the Schedule of Supplies/Services (Block 20). The price listed 
below shall include all labor and materials, overhead, and profit. GST, if applicable, will be priced 
separately as indicated in Section 2.0.  

The Government will pay the Contractor the fixed price per deliverable satisfactorily completed 
and accepted by the designated Point of Contact (POC). 

1.2. 

PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE 

Estimate Contract Start date: September 20, 2026, Estimate Contract End: September 30, 2027, 
Final Report Due: October 15, 2027. 

The performance period begins on the start date specified in the Notice to Proceed and continues 
through the contract end date above, encompassing all four deliverables described in the 
Schedule of Supplies/Services (Block 20).

DESCRIPTION/SPECIFICATION/WORK STATEMENT 

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES  

Project to design and deliver three one-day U.S.  Studies conferences in Canberra, Sydney, and 
Melbourne  to  strengthen  Australian  government  officials’  understanding  of  U.S.  history, 
democratic institutions, government structures, policymaking, foreign policy, and Australia–U.S. 
relations. 

2. SPECIFIC PROGRAM DELIVERABLES  

The  Contractor  shall  design  and  deliver  three  interactive,  one-day  U.S.  Studies  conferences  in 
Canberra, Sydney, and Melbourne for Australian government officials with little formal training 
or background on the United States, selected in coordination with the U.S. Embassy and relevant 
consulates. 

Each conference shall provide instruction across the following three thematic areas: 

1.  The constitutional and historical foundations of American democracy; 
2.  The roles and functions of the various components of the U.S. bureaucracy; and 
3.  An examination of current Washington policy settings. 

The conferences shall address the historical foundations of American national identity, the U.S. 
constitutional  and  political  framework,  strategic  policymaking  processes,  economic  and 
technological power, civil-military relations, the evolution of U.S. foreign policy since the end of 
the Cold War, U.S. engagement in the Pacific, the role of the United States in the international 
Order, and the future of Australia-U. S relations. 

Each  conference  shall  conclude  with  a  moderated  discussion  that  synthesizes  key  insights  and 
considers their implications for Australian policymakers. The Contractor shall provide qualified 
moderators  and  subject-matter  experts,  manage  conference  venues  and  logistics,  conduct 
participant  feedback  surveys  and  pre-  and  post-program  knowledge  assessments,  support  a 
networking reception following each conference, and submit an outcomes report for each program. 

The  conference  dates  will  be  determined  between  vendor  and  the  U.S.  Embassy  and  relevant 
consulates,  no  later  than  August  31,  2027,  and  will  conclude  with  an  embassy  or  consulate 
reception, bringing together participants with senior government representatives, members of the 
diplomatic  community,  business  leaders,  academics,  and  policy  experts  to  foster  dialogue, 
strengthen professional networks, and encourage ongoing collaboration subject to determination 
with American POC assigned. A final report to the U.S. Embassy will summarize the findings from 
the research and analysis, with an in-person briefing made available to officials from the Embassy.

SECTION 4 - EVALUATION FACTORS 

The Government intends to award a contract/purchase order resulting from this solicitation to the 
lowest priced, technically acceptable offeror/quoter who is a responsible contractor.  The 
evaluation process shall include the following: 

(a)  COMPLIANCE REVIEW.  The Government will perform an initial review of 

proposals/quotations received to determine compliance with the terms of the solicitation.  The 
Government may reject as unacceptable proposals/quotations that do not conform to the 
solicitation. 

(b) TECHNICAL ACCEPTABILITY.  Technical acceptability will include a review of 
past performance and experience as defined in Section 3, along with any technical information 
provided by the offeror with its proposal/quotation. 

(c)  PRICE EVALUATION.  The lowest price will be determined by multiplying the 
offered prices times the estimated quantities in “Prices - Continuation of SF-1449, block 23”, and 
arriving at a grand total, including all options.  The Government reserves the right to reject 
proposals that are unreasonably low or high in price. 

(d) RESPONSIBILITY DETERMINATION.  The Government will determine contractor 

responsibility by analyzing whether the apparent successful offeror complies with the 
requirements of FAR 9.1, including: 

•  Adequate financial resources or the ability to obtain them; 

•  Ability to comply with the required performance period, taking into consideration all 

existing commercial and governmental business commitments; 

•  Satisfactory record of integrity and business ethics; 

•  Necessary organization, experience, and skills or the ability to obtain them; 

•  Necessary equipment and facilities or the ability to obtain them; and 

•  Otherwise qualified and eligible to receive an award under applicable laws and 

regulations.
