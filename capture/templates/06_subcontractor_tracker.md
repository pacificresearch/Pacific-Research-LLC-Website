## Sourcing lanes (in order)
1. Local licensed trades near place of performance (state license
   boards, past federal sub award records on USASpending/FPDS)
2. Other small businesses in the NAICS active on SAM (potential
   similarly-situated subs — they COUNT toward PRG's 50%)
3. Staffing firms with the labor category on their bench
4. Distributors/OEM channel (supply plays — check nonmanufacturer rule)

## Per-agreement checklist
- [ ] Scope written as PWS-task subset, not "everything" (ostensible-sub risk)
- [ ] PRG retains PM, QC, customer interface, invoicing — on paper
- [ ] Flow-down clauses from compliance matrix D3 attached
- [ ] Payment terms: paid-when-paid or net-45 (protect working capital)
- [ ] COI naming PRG as additional insured where required
- [ ] Similarly-situated status documented if counted toward the 50%
- [ ] Commitment letter for use in proposal (firm, signed, dated)

## Status cadence
Re-touch every committed sub every 2 weeks until award decision.
Commitments and quotes go stale; a sub who ghosted after award is a
performance failure.
