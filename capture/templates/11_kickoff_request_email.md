# Kickoff request — email template

Send within **week 1** of award, from Andrew@pacificresearchllc.com.
⛔ Claude drafts; Andrew reviews and sends.

Purpose is not ceremony. It is to surface, on the record and early, the
handful of facts that otherwise get discovered late and expensively:
who the COR actually is, how invoices really get processed, what access
takes, and what the customer considers a good month.

---

**To:** [CO email] · **Cc:** [COR email, once known]
**Subject:** [Contract number] — Kickoff request and points of contact — Pacific Research Group LLC

Dear [Mr./Ms. Last name],

Thank you for the award of [contract number], [short title]. We are
prepared to begin performance on [PoP start date].

To ensure a clean start, may we schedule a brief kickoff at your
convenience in the next two weeks? We would use it to confirm:

1. **Points of contact** — the COR of record and the preferred channel
   for routine coordination versus formal correspondence.
2. **First deliverables** — priorities and sequencing for the opening
   period[, and the process for issuing the first task order].
3. **Access and onboarding** — background investigation submission,
   badging, and any system or site access, along with expected lead
   times so we can schedule around them.
4. **Invoicing** — confirmation of the payment office and submission
   method, so our first invoice arrives correctly the first time.
5. **Reporting** — format and cadence you prefer, and any templates you
   would like us to use.

We are ready to work to your schedule and can meet by phone or Teams.

Respectfully,

[signature block — `capture/library/boilerplate/company_data_block.md`]

---

## After the kickoff
- Minute it and send a short written confirmation of what was agreed —
  it becomes the record.
- Update the project calendar with anything new.
- Log the COR's stated preferences; performing to them is most of what
  a good CPARS rating measures.
