# Award vs. quote — diff checklist

Run this **before performance starts**, within 48 hours of the award
notice. The awarded contract is the governing document; your quote is
not. They are not always the same, and the differences are yours to
find.

Anything that differs goes to the CO **in writing** before you perform.
A discrepancy raised on day 2 is an administrative note. The same
discrepancy raised in month 3 is a dispute.
