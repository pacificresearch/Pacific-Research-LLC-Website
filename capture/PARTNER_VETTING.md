# Partner vetting standard (Andrew, 2026-11-20: "vet everyone. This is really important.")

Goal: never get caught up with the wrong people, and never carry a partner's bad record
(government complaints, exclusions, poor reviews) onto PRG.

## Preference
Principals with a master's degree or above are preferred; a PhD/doctorate is a plus. Record each
principal's highest degree *as published by the firm* and say it is unverified unless a licensing
board, university or other primary source confirms it. Degree level is a ranking factor, never a
substitute for the checks below.

## Tier 1 — before a first intro email (cheap, every firm)
- Certification: SBA certification directory shows the status the firm claims (SDVOSB/VOSB active).
- SAM.gov: active registration, not expired, and **not on the exclusions list**.
- A real, reachable principal and a working published address (never guessed).
- If a check cannot run (SAM quota 429, site down), the result is **UNVERIFIED**, never "clear".
  An unverified firm gets no send until the check runs.

## Tier 2 — before any NDA, teaming agreement, mentor agreement, or naming the firm to the government
Everything in Tier 1, plus:
- Past performance: federal awards (USAspending/FPDS), agencies, sizes; ask the firm for CPARS/PPQs
  (CPARS itself is not public).
- Complaints and risk: web/news search, GAO protest history, litigation, state business-registry
  standing, Better Business Bureau/online reviews, any debarment or suspension.
- SBA affiliation and size: confirm the firm is small in the relevant NAICS and not affiliated in a
  way that breaks a set-aside; confirm "similarly situated" status where PRG relies on it.
- People: published bios, education and licenses of key personnel (see Preference).
- Two references PRG contacts directly.
- Written OK from the firm before PRG names it in any document (same rail as intl Rail 2).

## Record
Each vetted firm gets a line: date, what was checked, source URL, result (CLEAR / FLAG / UNVERIFIED).
Anything FLAG goes to Andrew before another word is sent.
