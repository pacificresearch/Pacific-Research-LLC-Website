## THE ONE TO BID — W912LR26QA043

**PRARNG Medical Readiness Events (Periodic Health Assessment / Soldier
Readiness Processing)** · Puerto Rico Army National Guard, USPFO PR ·
Combined synopsis/solicitation · **Quotes due 3 Sep 2026, 16:00** (16
days) · [SAM](https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/aeafa4a4435f4a0e88fcfabe0ee11de3/view)

- **100% Small Business set-aside**, NAICS 621999 (All Other Misc. Health
  Care Services), size standard $20.5M · PSC Q201
- **Period of performance: 15 Sep 2026 – 24 Sep 2027 base, plus four
  12-month options and a 6-month extension.** That is a ~5.5-year
  vehicle, which is where the real value is — not the base year.
- Scope: physical examinations, dental evaluations, optometry screenings,
  immunizations, medical records updates, at Camp Santiago Joint Maneuver
  Training Center, Salinas, PR. Contractor provides all personnel,
  equipment, materials, supervision, and quality control.

**Why this one.** It is the highest-scoring notice in the run that sits
inside the founder's actual operating history rather than adjacent to it:
high-throughput medical screening events are precisely Operation Allies
Welcome (25K+ evacuees, interagency/CDC compliance) and the mass
testing/vaccination work. It is a set-aside PRG is eligible for, it is a
recurring multi-year vehicle, and the fulfillment model is the
aggregation model exactly — PRG primes, sources licensed clinicians,
owns QC/invoicing/compliance.

**Two corrections to the matcher's own scoring — do not price off them:**
1. It tagged this **founder-delivered, solo: Yes, GM 40–60%**. That is
   wrong. The PWS requires a *team* of licensed medical, dental, nursing,
   and allied science professionals. This is a **staffing / professional-
   services aggregation** play: realistic band **20–35%**, not 40–60%.
2. **Puerto Rico clinical licensure** is the real sourcing constraint —
   MDs/DOs or PA/NPs, dentists, optometrists, RNs and techs must be
   PR-licensed. Not a firm-licensure kill (the licenses ride with key
   personnel), but it decides who can be sourced and how fast. Verify
   before committing to the staffing plan.

## Matcher follow-ups this run exposed

1. **Fixed today:** SAM returns set-aside code `NONE` / "No Set aside
   used" for unrestricted notices; `classify_setaside` only recognised an
   empty code, so full-and-open work was marked ineligible and scored 0.
   Fixed with a regression test. Part of the 38 unrestricted candidates
   above were previously invisible.
2. **Open:** the solo/founder-delivered classifier is over-optimistic on
   multi-discipline clinical scopes (W912LR26QA043) and on engineering
   assessment scopes (W912LR26QA040). Both were tagged solo + 40–60%
   margin when neither is deliverable by one person. Margin bands
   downstream inherit the error, so this misprices bids. Worth a fix
   before the next pricing run.
