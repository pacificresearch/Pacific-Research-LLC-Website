# Sending authority — what Claude may send without asking

**Granted by Andrew, 2026-11-17**, for the account
`andrew@pacificresearchllc.com`. This file is the standing record. A
scheduled run that needs to know whether it may send reads this, not a
conversation.

## May be sent without asking

- Responses to **sources sought** notices and **RFIs** (nonbinding market
  research).
- **Clarification questions** to a contracting officer, where the notice
  permits questions and by the channel it names.
- **Supplier and teaming inquiries**, and **requests for quotes** to
  suppliers.
- One **follow-up** per unanswered message, 5 to 7 business days later.

## Never sent without Andrew

- A **quote**, a **price**, or a rough-order-of-magnitude figure to the
  Government. A quote is a binding offer.
- A **proposal** or any submission to a solicitation.
- **Certifications or representations** made on PRG's behalf.
- **Agreements** of any kind: NDAs, teaming agreements, exclusivity,
  subcontracts, purchase orders.
- Anything that **names a partner** before a signed agreement exists.
- Anything that **argues** with a contracting officer's decision
  (CLAUDE.md standing order).

## Checks before every send

1. **Read the notice's own instructions.** If it requires a portal, a
   form, a file format, a subject line, or prohibits email, do that
   instead. If it requires an **attachment**, stop: the Outlook connector
   cannot attach files, so prepare the draft and the file and hand both
   to Andrew.
2. **Search Sent Items by notice number first.** A hit means skip. Never
   a second copy, a corrected copy, or an unprompted follow-up.
3. **Every factual claim traces to** `capture/library/company_profile.md`
   or the notice. Individual experience of the Managing Director is
   labelled as individual, never as PRG contract performance.
4. **Honor do-not-contact.** Check the supplier and contact records
   before sending; an opt-out is permanent.
5. **Record it** in the contracting database the same run: the message,
   the stage change, the follow-up date.

No message is sent to meet a count. If nothing qualifies, nothing goes.

## Volume and approval (Andrew, 2026-11-17)

- **Target: about 50 outbound messages per business day.** Andrew's words:
  "I don't like 4 or 5, I like 50." Most of that volume is teaming and
  supplier outreach; a contracting officer only ever receives a response a
  notice invites.
- **Approved as a batch, not one by one.** "I approve all of them all at
  once." Messages inside the boundary above go without a per-message
  check-in.
- **The target does not lower the bar.** Every message names a specific
  notice or a specific reason for that firm, uses facts from the company
  profile, and tells the recipient they can decline further contact. If
  fewer than 50 firms qualify on a given day, fewer go out and the report
  says how many and why.
- **Who is worth writing to.** On an SDVOSB set-aside the partner must be a
  certified SDVOSB (similarly situated) or the work must be done by PRG's own
  W2 hires; on a total small business set-aside any small business
  qualifies. A requirement one specialist firm would perform end to end is a
  pass for PRG as prime (CLAUDE.md kill 6): write to that firm about teaming
  with PRG as a member, not about subcontracting to PRG.
- **Send window:** 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the recipient's time zone where
  practical.
