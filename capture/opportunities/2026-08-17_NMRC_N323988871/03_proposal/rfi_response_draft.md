## 2. Capabilities Statement

**Understanding and ability to perform the ENTIRE scope.** PRG
understands this requirement provides BioMed/Biosafety/Equipment
Inventory and Accountability support to NMRC-Frederick's military
medical research mission over a potential 30-month period: Level II
scheduled maintenance (inspection, PM, calibration, scheduled parts
replacement) on medical and research equipment documented in DMLSS;
electrical safety inspections; acceptance inspection of newly acquired
medical, dental, and research equipment; equipment inventory and
accountability per NAVMED P-5132 and current BUMED logistics guidance
including annual inventory and monthly GE-R reporting; warranty and
service-agreement tracking in DMLSS; purchase-package technical review;
operator training; storage/disposition tagging with DRMO/DLA
coordination; unable-to-locate reporting; and monthly progress
reporting. PRG is prepared to perform the entire scope and all PWS
tasks with qualified BMET personnel under prime-level management, QC,
and reporting.

**Directly relevant leadership and delivery capability.** PRG's
Managing Director is a U.S. military veteran and AAMI Certified
Biomedical Equipment Technician (CBET) with USAF biomedical equipment
training (4A2X5) who has personally managed $2M+ in biomedical asset
infrastructure at a >98% preventive-maintenance and compliance rate
aligned to Joint Commission and DoD accreditation standards, within
military treatment and research environments. PRG is actively
recruiting credential-verified BMET personnel for this requirement
(national postings live), with candidates screened against military
BMET training, manufacturer training, or CBET certification, DMLSS
familiarity, and the ability to obtain SECRET (Tier 3) eligibility and
laboratory occupational health clearances.

**Responsibility (FAR 9.104-1).** PRG confirms it meets the general
standards of responsibility: adequate financial resources for this
requirement, ability to comply with the proposed performance schedule,
a satisfactory record of integrity and business ethics, the necessary
organization and technical skills, and eligibility to receive award.
No exclusions; no delinquent federal debt.

**SAM registration.** PRG confirms current and active SAM.gov
registration (UEI J585TLDV1CH1, CAGE 1Z9B6).
