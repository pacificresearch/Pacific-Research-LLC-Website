                                    ATTACHMENT 4
                            CLIN 0003: Exercise Support Specialist
                              Contractor Qualification Criteria

NCETR, through funding provided by Department of the Army (DA) G-38, conducts a Military
Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) and Public Safety Bomb Squad (PSBS) interoperability
exercise, at multiple sites throughout the country, fostering an exchange of tools, techniques and
procedures between the two related, but different, missions.

Technical Proficiency:
Exercise Support Specialists should be capable of:

   1.    Conducting all EOD related duties during the execution of the exercise.

   2.    Monitoring and guiding explosives operations and actions during the exercise for the
         safety of all participants and staff, for use in Hot Wash and After Action Reviews, and
         to facilitate the latest technological threats in the Improvised Explosive Device (IED)
         arena.

   3.    Supporting other interests at the exercise such as providing explosives control,
         intelligence generation, and logistical support for site development and execution.

   4.    Supporting law enforcement operations/exercises with downrange support of explosive
         operations and creating the scenarios and documenting the results with technical
         proficiency.

   5.    Training the Observer/Controllers (OC) in the application of intelligence, explosive
         operations, and post blast scene investigation.

   6.    Training the OCs in the proper use of technical equipment to support the Exercise,
         therefore, they must be experienced law enforcement instructors.

   7.    Physically lifting heavy equipment (up to 50 lbs and carrying it up to 50 feet) and
         operating in remote areas in travel status for up to 15-30 days.

   8.    Selecting, installing, maintaining and troubleshooting/repairing technical equipment
         used to support the Exercise.

   9.    Supporting live fire explosive operations, and providing a vast working knowledge of
         explosives defeat operations.

   10.   Organizing and leading a team comprised of evaluators and operators from multiple
         personnel from Federal Agencies; Military and local law enforcement.

   Education/Experience and Certification Requirements:

   11.   A Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university with a minimum of ten
         years subject matter related work experience, law enforcement field and/or training
