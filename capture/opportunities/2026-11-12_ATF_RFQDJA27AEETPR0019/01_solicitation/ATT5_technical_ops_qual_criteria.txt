                                    ATTACHMENT 5
                         CLIN 0004: Technical Operations Specialist


                               Contractor Qualification Criteria


NCETR, through funding provided by Department of the Army (DA) G-38, conducts a Military
Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) and Public Safety Bomb Squad (PSBS) interoperability
exercise, at multiple sites throughout the country, fostering an exchange of tools, techniques and
procedures between the two related, but different, missions.

Technical Proficiency:

   1. Maintaining cutting edge methods of capturing and documenting EOD/PSBS actions
      during the exercise for the safety of all participants and staff. This documentation is used
      in Hot Wash, After Action Reviews, and facilitates the latest technological threats in the
      Improvised Explosive Device (IED) arena.

   2. Supporting other interests at the exercise such as providing Wi-Fi in remote training sites
      to allow for reporting via Mobile BATS from each scenario lane.

   3. Selecting, installing, maintaining, and troubleshooting/repairing technical equipment used
      to support the Exercise.

   4. Transferring video and other data to a medium suitable for distribution to exercise
      participants.

   5. Organizing and leading a team comprised of Technical Operations personnel from
      Federal Agencies; Military and local law enforcement.

   6. Training the Observer/Controllers in the proper use of technical equipment to support the
      Exercise, therefore, they must be experienced law enforcement Technical Operations
      Specialists.

   7. Supporting law enforcement operations/exercises with technical proficiency by capturing
      and documenting exercise actions via technologies including, but not limited to, fixed
      cameras; body worn cameras; establishing Wi-Fi systems in remote, unique sites and new
      emerging technologies such as Unmanned Aerial Surveillance (UAS) platforms.

   8. Managing the communications of the various ATF Mobile Command Vehicles; this
      requires a strong background in ATF Communications.

   9. Working around live explosives with full knowledge of blast effects and their impact on
      the placement of cameras and other devices used to capture and document exercise
      operations.

   10. Protecting cameras and other devices used to capture and document exercise operations,
       including, but not limited to, ballistic enclosures.

Education/Experience and Certification Requirements:

   11. Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university with a minimum of ten years
       of subject matter related work experience (i.e., law enforcement and military, with a
       major portion of this experience in the explosives and investigative operations arena) -
       preferred.
                                                   Or
      A high school education with a diploma or a GED equivalency certificate and 15 years of
      general law enforcement/IOI/military/or field related experience may be substituted for a
      Bachelor’s degree.
                                                  Or
      A Graduate degree, with a minimum of 5 years working, instructing, or conducting
      research in the subject area at a level exceeding the students to be taught.


   12. Special consideration will be given to those with:
          • Past experience working on Raven’s Challenge Exercises, or
          • Experience as an ATF Tactical Operations Officer or Technical Surveillance
              Specialist
          • Extensive knowledge of current state of the art surveillance techniques.

   13. Successful completion of a Technical Operations Specialist training course or
       demonstrated experience providing technical operation support in a professional setting.

   14. Must have been a Certified Technical Investigator UBIQUITI trained in radio systems for
       transmission of video and View Commander.

   15. Provide approach to maintain the currency of subject matter expertise and updates to
       continuing education and required certifications.

   16. Provide verifying documentation, upon request, (e.g., certificate of successful completion
       or attendance) demonstrating maintenance of subject matter currency through recent
       continuing education and training activities in the subject matter being taught. Subject
       matter currency may also be validated through the publication of subject matter related
       articles and research in nationally recognized professional journals or trade magazines.
