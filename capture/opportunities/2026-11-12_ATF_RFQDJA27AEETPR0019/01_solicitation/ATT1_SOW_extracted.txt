                                    STATEMENT OF WORK

1.0      OVERVIEW

The Bureau of Alcohol Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives (ATF), National Center for Explosives
Training and Research (NCETR) is issuing this Statement of Work (SOW) for Explosives
Specialist, Fire / Arson Specialist, Exercise Support Specialist, Technical Operations Specialist,
Academy Canine Trainer and NORT Canine Trainer, (herein referred to as “Contractor”) with the
intent to issue multiple Blanket Purchase Agreements (BPA) with fixed daily rates. ATF
anticipates opportunities to provide services will be rotated among successful contractors after
consideration of availability, rates, geographic location and positive performance ratings.

Once a contractor has been awarded a BPA, ATF is not obligated to select that contractor to
provide services for a specific course or to continue to use a contractor following BPA award. The
selection of a contractor under the BPA is within ATF’s discretion and authority and is not subject
to protest or dispute procedures.

Under this agreement, ATF may use the negotiated rates to apply to actual service time or for time
spent developing content for new or updated training. It is not the intent of this agreement to
procure the services of placement companies with a pool of available instructors. BPAs will be
awarded on a one-to-one, individual basis.

In support of ATF’s Strategic Plan and mission objectives, NCETR is dedicated to supporting ATF
personnel, law enforcement, military, and industry partners through the dissemination of ATF’s
unique knowledge and expertise to prevent terrorism, reduce violent crime, and protect the public.
NCETR’s training mission is to:

      1. Assess, design, deliver and evaluate innovative training programs,
      2. Facilitate a creative learning environment that fosters continuous learner development,
         and
      3. Create an adaptable workforce capable of meeting current and future mission
         requirements.

2.0      SCOPE

ATF has a number of responsibilities with regard to the assessment, development, delivery, and
evaluation of training programs and is seeking to obtain contractors to further support their
training and capabilities exercise efforts. ATF delivers a number of law enforcement related
topics, at multiple locations, to a variety of students including Federal, State and local law
enforcement personnel, ATF industry operations, military personnel, federal government
civilians, and other public safety related personnel. Program Managers must maintain the ability
to update and change curricula on a regular basis, so the requirements for specific skills sets and
the frequency of their use may vary greatly depending on current staffing levels and types of
courses or exercises being offered.

3.0      CONTRACTOR REQUIREMENTS
Contractors acknowledge meeting the following minimum qualifications upon proposal
submission:

      1. Contractors:

             a. Must be able to travel to the training/exercise site as dictated by the agreement.
                This may involve travel domestically or internationally depending on the
                program.
             b. Must have a flexible work schedule that meets the needs of the training/exercise
                being provided or developed.
             c. Must be able to pass a background investigation as required by the training
                program or training facility (see section 3.4 for more information).
             d. Must have and maintain an active registration in SAM.gov.
             e. Must be willing to volunteer for random drug testing.
             f. Must have access to the internet and maintain a working email address for access
                to required on-line applications and to receive contractor notifications and
                announcements.

      2. Contractors must not have been:

             a. Convicted of a felony offense.
             b. Convicted for an offense involving violence against a law enforcement officer.
             c. Convicted for an offense involving rape, child brutality, or child molestation.
             d. Convicted for an offense of either drug or firearms sales or trafficking activities.
             e. Convicted for an offense that, if revealed, would reflect poorly upon the Federal
                Government or would otherwise present a threat to the safety and security of
                employees.
             f. Must not be currently subject to disciplinary action by an employer, released from
                a position as a result of a disciplinary action, or have resigned from a position
                prior to the culmination of a disciplinary investigation.
             g. Must not be a prohibited person in regards to explosives usage and possession as
                per Federal statutes.
             h. Must not be a prohibited person in regards to possessing and transferring firearms
                as per Federal statutes.

3.     Contractors must be able to demonstrate professional behavior and expertise that meet ATF
       and the National Center for Explosives Training and Research standards.

4.     Explosives Instructors (CLIN 0001), Fire/Arson Instructors (CLIN 0002), Canine Trainers

       training, which includes being intimately familiar with the lesson plan, its requirements and

4.0    ASSUMPTIONS AND CONSTRAINTS

4.1    Copyright Clause

Contractors may not utilize any materials or information provided by ATF (unless publicly
available) including regulations, policies, directives, correspondence, standard operating
procedures (SOPs), photographs or videos, or any classified, law enforcement sensitive, or
confidential information for the purposes of any other production, contract or project. Any
lesson plans, curriculum, agenda, photographs, videos or materials prepared by the contractor for
ATF under this contract shall be deemed ATF proprietary data and as such is the sole property of
the United States Government. The contractor may not copyright, resell, share, present, or
transmit any materials or information provided to the Instructor by ATF without the prior written
permission of ATF.

4.2    Expert Witness/Industry Consultant Clause

The contractor is prohibited from contracting to testify against ATF or any Federal governmental
entity while contracted to provide goods or services to ATF under this or any other Department
of Justice contract. Further, the contractor agrees that he/she is prohibited from testifying as an
expert or contractual witness against ATF or any governmental entity on any matter that requires
the contractor to rely upon or reference any lesson plans, curricula, or courses prepared by the
contractor pursuant to this contract, or upon any materials or information provided to contractor
by ATF, that is not otherwise publicly available. The contractor may not share or transmit to his
or her agents, employee(s), employer(s) any of the aforementioned materials, information, lesson
plans, curricula or courses for the purpose of testimony as an expert or contractual witness in any
proceeding; administrative, civil or criminal.

Contractors generally are not prohibited from acting as consultants for industry Federal firearms
licensees (FFLs), or Federal explosives licensees or permittees (FELs), lobbying groups,
associations, or any private entity to assist with improving compliance with Federal laws and
regulations. However, the contractor is prohibited from serving as a consultant or advisor, to
industry FFLs, FELs, lobbying groups, associations, or any private entity on any matter related to
any ATF adverse actions, to include administrative hearings, warning conferences, or revocation
hearings while contracted to provide goods or services to ATF under this or any other
Department of Justice contract.

4.3    Professional Insurance Coverage

Contractors are responsible for acquiring and maintaining their own professional insurance for
all work provided in relation to this agreement. In the event of injuries as a result of
participation in training, demonstrations, or other work provided under this agreement,
contractors are not compensated by the ATF or the U.S. Department of Justice through workers
compensation, employee insurance, automobile insurance, rental insurance, travel insurance, or
any other similar mechanism. Further, any damage to any equipment or materials used or owned
by the contractor employed in work conducted under this agreement is the responsibility of the

4.4    Background Investigation

All contractors are subject to a background investigation. Before an agreement can be placed by
ATF, the contractor will be required to complete a personnel security package which includes
forms SF 85P, Questionnaire for Public Trust Positions; ATF F 8620.26, Credit Release; ATF F
8620.27, Non-Disclosure Agreement; and questionnaires regarding drug use and financial
declarations. The package also includes two fingerprint cards that must be completed and
provided along with two passport type photos. BPAs will not be awarded without a completed
successful adjudication. Failure to comply with requests for background investigations will result
in immediate termination of the proposal/agreement.

Additionally, contractors are required to provide personal information electronically through the
Department of Justice e-QIP on-line application. Contractors will receive a unique link into
E-QIP via email, where they will be required to provide the requested information in the system.
Contractors are to print out the specified signature pages from e-QIP and include the signed
documents in the personnel security package when it is completed and returned.
Failure to complete e-QIP and submit the personnel security package in a timely manner may
result in the immediate termination of the proposal/agreement.

4.5    Registration Requirements

A SAM registration is required for any entity to bid on and get paid for federal contracts or to
receive federal funds. These include for profit businesses, nonprofits, government contractors,
government subcontractors, state governments, and local municipalities. SAM.gov registration is
available at US Federal Contractor Registration, www.usfcr.com. It is the responsibility of the
contractor to have and maintain a SAM registration. Failure to have or maintain a SAM
registration will result in immediate termination of the proposal/agreement.

4.6    Ordering and Invoicing Requirements

Ordering

When an ATF program manager identifies a need for a contractor, he/she will contact potential
contractors to determine their availability. Typically, ATF provides a lead time of sixty (60)
days. However, when a need to backfill a requirement or some other emergency requirement
occurs, ATF may make contractor availability requests with much shorter lead times.

For routine requests, ATF will allow the contractor up to two (2) days from the time of request to
respond affirmatively, before seeking a contractor from another source. For non-routine requests,
the ATF Training Manager will determine the amount of available response time allowed on a
case-by-case basis and provide this information to the contractor at the time of the request. No
contractor will have recourse against ATF when situations dictate contractor scheduling outside
