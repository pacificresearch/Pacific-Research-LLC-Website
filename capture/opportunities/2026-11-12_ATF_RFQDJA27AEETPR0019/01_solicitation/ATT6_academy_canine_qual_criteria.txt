                         CLIN 0005: Canine Trainer – NCD Academy

                                Contractor Qualification Criteria


Technical Proficiency:

1. Instructors must possess law enforcement, investigational application of detector canines,
   explosives training skills, experience with various canine training methodologies, or have
   unique experience, education, and training to support ATF’s commitment to train superior
   explosives and accelerant detector canines. These trainings and experiences are highly
   regarded skill sets and are required for specialty programs at NCETR.

2. Areas of expertise desired by Canine Trainers include, but are not limited to:

   •   Food Reward Training Methodology            •   Canine Basic and Emergency Care
   •   Explosives and Accelerant Detection         •   Nutrition/Fitness Requirements for Canine
       Canine Training                             •   Explosives/Accelerant Recordkeeping
   •   Investigational Application of Detector     •   Explosives Classification
       Canines                                     •   Explosives Laws and Regulations
   •   Canine Handling, On and Off leash           •   Federal Explosives Laws and Regulations
   •   Various Canine Training
       Methodologies

3. ATF uses a variety of instructional methods, including classroom lectures and
   demonstrations, practical exercises (PE), and odor recognition testing. The contractor shall
   provide input for the revision or amendment of the lectures, demonstrations, and exercises to
   provide updated materials, as appropriate and necessary for the course requirements.

4. Many courses require the instructor to provide both classroom as well as practical instruction.

5. Contractor rates will be used for all services including, but not limited to, instruction, canine
   care, vehicle maintenance, practical instruction, and course development.

6. Contractor shall be flexible and available seven (7) days per week to include holidays if
   warranted.

7. Exceptions may be made to contractor instructors who possess unique qualifications based on
   industry experience.

Education/Experience and Certification Requirements:

8. Minimum five (5) years’ experience with and proficient in “food reward” training
   methodology, in canine explosive and accelerant detection

9. Minimum fine (5) years’ experience in law enforcement, particularly the investigational
   application of detector canines.

10. Minimum five (5) years’ experience with the recognition, use and handling of explosives as
    well as a complete understanding of Federal and state storage regulations.

11. Minimum five (5) years’ experience with various canine detector training methodologies, to
    include explosives and accelerant detection.

12. Minimum three (3) years’ experience operating all classroom multimedia devices.

13. Shall assist in setup and cleaning of training site and training courses. Also, assist with
    transportation of training materials to sites, if warranted.

14. Must be able to provide basic and emergency first aid when needed, to include CPR if
    warranted. Must utilize prescribed safety methods and procedures in handling trained and
    untrained canines in kennel areas, public environments, and veterinary treatment facilities.
    Required to report all injuries, canine bites, and safety issues immediately.

15. Special consideration may be given to those who possess experience instructing
    ATF/NCETR training programs within three years of proposal submission.

16. Canine Trainers must have documentation of training experience, to include certificates of
    completion, curriculum vitae and/or resume.
