   CLIN 0002: Fire/Arson Specialist - Law Enforcement Instructor/Program Technical Specialist

                                   Contractor Qualification Criteria


Technical Proficiency:

 1. Instructors must possess law enforcement fire and arson investigation, fire and arson
    instructor/technical expertise, or have unique experience, education, and training to support ATF’s
    fire and arson enforcement mission. These trainings and experiences are highly regarded skill sets
    and are required for specialty programs at NCETR. and should possess

 2. Areas of expertise desired in fire and arson instructors includes, but is not limited to:

     •    Fire Science                                    •     Fire Protection Systems
     •    Fire Chemistry                                  •     Evidence Documentation, Collection,
     •    Thermodynamics                                        and Preservation
     •    Thermometry                                     •     Electricity and Electrical Systems
     •    Fire Dynamics                                   •     Criminalistics and Forensic Science
     •    Explosion Dynamics                              •     Federal & State Fire and Arson Laws and
     •    Computer Fire Modeling                                Regulations
     •    Fire Investigation                              •     Fire Investigation Techniques
     •    Fire Analysis                                   •     Litigation related to Fire Investigation
     •    Fire Investigation Methodology                  •     Fire Scene Health and Safety
     •    Fire Investigation Technology                   •     Fire Suppression
     •    Hazardous Materials                             •     Instrumentation
     •    Failure Analysis and Analytical Tools

 3. ATF uses a variety of instructional methods, including classroom lectures and demonstrations,
    practical exercises (PE), and live range operations. The contractor shall provide input for the
    revision or amendment of the lectures, demonstrations, and exercises to provide updated materials,
    as appropriate and necessary for the course requirements.

 4. Many courses require the instructor to provide both classroom as well as range instruction.

 5. Contractor rates will be used for all services including but not limited to instruction, range activities
    and course development.

 6. Exceptions may be made to contractor instructors who possess unique qualifications based on fire
    and arson investigation industry experience, litigation of fire and arson related legal proceedings,
    research and development experience, or graduate level education in fire related disciplines,
    engineering, and scientific related disciplines.

 Education/Experience and Certification Requirements:

 7. A Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university with a minimum of ten years subject
    matter related work experience, law enforcement field and/or training experience with a major

8. Special consideration may be given to:
      • Those who possess experience instructing for ATF/NCETR fire and arson training programs
          within three years of proposal submission
      • ATF Certified Fire Investigators
      • ATF Certified Explosives Specialists
      • Completion of ATF Advanced Origin & Cause and Courtroom Testimony (AOCCT) school

      • Documented experience with providing instruction for ATF fire and arson training held at
          the National Center for Explosives Training and Research (NCETR), National Fire Academy
          (NFA), Fire Research Laboratory (FRL), Federal Law Enforcement Training Center
          (FLETC), or International Law Enforcement Academy (ILEA)
      • Documented experience providing instruction for the International Association of Arson
          Investigators (IAAI) will also meet the requirement


9. Successful completion of an instructor training course or demonstrated experience providing
   instruction in either a professional or academic classroom setting.

10. Provide approach to maintain the currency of subject matter expertise and updates to continuing
    education and required certifications.

11. Provide verifying documentation, upon request, (e.g., certificate of successful completion or
    attendance) demonstrating maintenance of subject matter currency through recent continuing
    education and training activities in the subject matter being taught. Subject matter currency may
    also be validated through the publication of subject matter related articles and research in nationally
    recognized professional journals or trade magazines.
