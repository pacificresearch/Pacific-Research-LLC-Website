## Why this one is the selection

- **Price is not rated.** §6 of the RFQ: *"Though price is not rated,
  price reasonableness will be determined by the contracting officer in
  accordance with FAR 12.204."* Award turns on technical acceptability
  (meet/exceed) per labor category. PRG's management margin is not
  competed away — the single most important fact in this file.
- **No past performance is not disqualifying.** Factor 2: *"If an
  offeror has no recent/relevant past performance, they must state so
  and will be rated neutral. Non submittal of past performance will not
  affect offeror's award determination."* This is a genuine first-contract
  opportunity.
- **Multiple award, not winner-take-all.** The Government "reserves the
  right to issue as many or as few BPAs to offerors as determined by the
  needs of the Government."
- **Annual on-ramp.** Windows reopen Oct 15–31 in 2027/2028/2029/2030. A
  miss this year is recoverable; an award this year compounds.
- **Limitation on subcontracting is not a constraint here.** Small
  business set-aside: sole-proprietor 1099 instructor SMEs are
  similarly-situated small business concerns, so the 50% services cap
  under FAR 52.219-14 is not binding on this structure.

## Fulfillment model

PRG primes; sources credentialed 1099 instructor SMEs per labor category;
owns recruiting, credential verification, Public Trust sponsorship, QC,
scheduling, invoicing and CO relationship. Fulfillment model:
**professional services** (target band 30–50%, floor 22%).

Bid only the labor categories PRG can evidence a qualified instructor
for. Quoting a category with no identified SME risks an Unacceptable
rating on that category — and the rating is per-category, so a weak
category does not contaminate a strong one.
