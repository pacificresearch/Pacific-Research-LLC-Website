## 1. Understanding of the Requirement (~1 pg)
The VA Cooperative Studies Program's SMART, co-located with the CSP
Clinical Research Pharmacy Coordinating Center in Albuquerque, safeguards
GCP compliance across a portfolio of ~25 ongoing multi-site trials
(300–400 investigative sites; 8–12 trials actively monitored each year)
conducted at VA and affiliated non-VA facilities. SMART's authorized
staffing does not cover the monitoring workload; this requirements
contract closes that gap with qualified contract CRQAs who execute
study-specific monitoring plans — site initiation, routine, and closeout
visits, delivered on-site (with travel) or virtually, plus remote
key-data review and GCP Program Quality Support — under SMART SOPs and
AMPs, with post-visit deliverables on fixed timelines (routine reports
10 calendar days from last visit day). We understand success is measured
not by visits completed but by audit-ready documentation, consistency
with the CRQA Monitoring Plan, and zero surprises for CSP leadership:
findings communicated early, escalated correctly, and closed.

We further understand the operational realities: schedules shift with
study status; monitors must stand ready across time zones; VA
credentialing, PIV, and privacy/security rules govern site access; and
every monitor must arrive already fluent in the trial's protocol,
operations manual, and the references in PWS 3.0 (ICH E6(R2), 45 CFR 46,
38 CFR 16, 21 CFR 312/812, VHA directives, CSP/SMART SOPs).

## 2. Technical Approach — PWS 4.0 (General Scope) (~1 pg)
- Named, qualified CRQAs assigned per task order; identification within
  30 days of notification (PWS 4.2), drawn from our credential-verified
  monitor bench (see Staffing Plan).
- Each monitor onboarded to the specific trial: monitoring plan,
  protocol/ops manual review, SMART SOP/AMP alignment, and a readiness
  check with the Lead CRQA before first visit.
- Scheduling discipline: visits coordinated through SMART-designated
  channels; changes documented with CSP program-official confirmation
  (PWS 6.2.3.2) and reflected in invoicing records.
