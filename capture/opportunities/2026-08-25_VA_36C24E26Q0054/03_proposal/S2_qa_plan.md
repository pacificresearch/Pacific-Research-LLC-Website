# SECTION 2 — DRAFT QUALITY ASSURANCE PLAN (excluded from page count)

## 1. Purpose & alignment
QA plan for CRQA monitoring services under 36C24E26Q0054, aligned to the
PWS/QASP, SMART SOPs and AMPs, and ICH E6(R2). Objective: every visit
executed to the study-specific CRQA Monitoring Plan and every
deliverable audit-ready on first submission.

## 2. Quality standards & metrics
| Deliverable / activity | Standard | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Visit conduct | Per CRQA Monitoring Plan + SMART SOPs | 100% plan adherence |
| Routine visit report + investigator summary | ≤10 calendar days from last visit day | 100% on-time |
| Report quality | Complete, accurate, audit-ready; Lead CRQA review before release | ≥95% accepted without rework |
| Monitor credentials | PWS 7.3 documentation current | 100%, tracked w/ 90-day alerts |
| Visit scheduling | Confirmed via SMART channels; changes documented per PWS 6.2.3.2 | 100% documented |

## 3. QC method
- **Pre-visit**: checklist verification (credentials current, monitoring
  plan reviewed, prior findings staged, site logistics confirmed).
- **In-visit**: standardized working papers per SMART AMPs.
- **Post-visit**: Lead CRQA reviews 100% of reports before submission
  in year one; sampling may relax only with Government concurrence.
- **Trend review**: monthly internal review of findings, timeliness,
  and site feedback; corrective actions logged with owner + due date.

## 4. Deficiency handling
Any missed standard → root-cause within 5 business days → corrective
action → verification at next deliverable. Repeat deficiencies escalate
to Managing Director with process change, not just correction. The
Government sees issues from us first: proactive notice to SMART
leadership of any deliverable at risk, before the due date.

## 5. Records
Visit files, reports, credential documentation, and QC checklists
retained per VHA Directive 1907.01 and CSP/SMART records requirements,
available to the Government on request.
