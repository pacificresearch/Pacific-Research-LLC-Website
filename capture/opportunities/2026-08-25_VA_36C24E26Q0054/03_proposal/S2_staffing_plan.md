# SECTION 2 — STAFFING PLAN (HARD 2-PAGE LIMIT)

## 1. Staffing model
Two-tier bench sized to SMART's stated workload (8–12 actively
monitored trials/yr; 25-trial portfolio):
- **Tier 1 — Lead CRQA (Managing Director)**: ACRP-certified, GCP/HSP
  documented; performs monitoring visits directly and quality-reviews
  every deliverable before release to SMART. Single accountable point
  of contact for scheduling, escalation, and task-order response.
- **Tier 2 — Credential-verified monitor bench**: qualified CRQAs
  (PWS 7.3-compliant, documentation on file BEFORE assignment) engaged
  W2 or 1099 as task-order volume requires. Active recruiting pipeline
  already running (public careers page + national job boards) with
  credential screening against PWS 7.3 as the application gate.

## 2. Task-order response (PWS 4.2 — 30 days)
Day 0–3 confirm scope/monitoring plan → Day 3–10 match monitor(s) from
bench, verify credential currency → Day 10–20 trial-specific onboarding
(protocol, ops manual, SMART SOPs/AMPs) → Day 20–30 readiness check by
Lead CRQA, submission of monitor identification + documentation to
SMART. Surge: bench depth target = 2x concurrent-visit load; recruiting
pipeline refreshed continuously, not per-award.

## 3. Continuity & quality of staffing
- Credential expiration tracking (GCP refreshers, cert renewals) with
  90-day advance alerts; no monitor performs with lapsed credentials —
  consistent with our Certifying Statement.
- Cross-briefed backup monitor designated per active study; handover
  protocol (files, findings log, site contacts) for any transition.
- VA credentialing/PIV initiated at assignment, tracked to completion
  before first site visit.
- Limitations on Subcontracting (VAAR 852.219-75): W2 monitors and
  similarly-situated SDVOSB subcontractors constitute the required
  performance share; compliance tracked per invoice period.
