## 3. Labor basis — why this rate

**Personnel bar (PWS 7.3.5, 7.3.6):** minimum **10 years** GCP clinical
trial monitoring of research sites, **plus 5 years** GCP monitoring or
auditing of **federally funded** research — and monitoring a VA site for
a *commercial* sponsor expressly does not count. Bachelor's/advanced
degree in a science or health field, or licensure, or CCRA. This is a
senior, scarce profile and it sets the wage.

**Anchor: federal equivalence.** The contract incorporates **FAR
52.222-42, Statement of Equivalent Rates for Federal Hires**, so GS
equivalence is the natural reasonableness frame for the CO. That
profile maps to **GS-13**.

> 2026 GS Rest-of-U.S. table (1% GS increase + 17.06% locality,
> effective January 2026): **GS-13 Step 1 = $106,437**, Step 5 =
> $120,629. GS-12 Step 5 = $101,443.
> Source: OPM Salary Table 2026-RUS.

**No SCA wage-determination floor binds these monitors.** 52.222-49
(*Place of Performance Unknown*) is incorporated and no WD is attached;
degreed/certified monitors with 10 years' experience are bona fide
professional employees exempt under 29 CFR 541.300. Documented here so
the position is on the record.

**Assumed base salary: $105,000** — sits between GS-12 Step 5 and GS-13
Step 1 and inside the market band for a senior/lead CRA.

## 4. The binding constraint is the ceiling, not the margin band

CLAUDE.md puts professional services at 30–50% GM. **That band does not
fit this vehicle.** Pricing the Government's own estimated quantities at
a 30% margin breaches the stated $2,000,000 maximum:

| Hourly | Block (8 h) | GM | 3-yr labor | Head-room vs $2M |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $95 | $760 | 10.5% | $1,681,500 | $318,500 |
| $100 | $800 | 15.0% | $1,770,000 | $230,000 |
| **$105** | **$840** | **19.0%** | **$1,858,500** | **$141,500** |
| $110 | $880 | 22.7% | $1,947,000 | $53,000 |
| $115 | $920 | 26.1% | $2,035,500 | **−$35,500 ✗** |
| $120 | $960 | 29.2% | $2,124,000 | **−$124,000 ✗** |

Travel is Government-set, but it consumes the same ceiling. Q&A #14 puts
the split at ~3:1 remote:on-site, so roughly 25 on-site trips a year; at
~$1,600 a trip that is ~$40,000/yr, **~$120,000 over three years**.

**Ceiling test at $105 / $840:** $1,858,500 labor + ~$120,000 travel =
**$1,978,500 against a $2,000,000 maximum — a 99% fit.** The Government
almost certainly sized the ceiling around these quantities at
approximately this rate, which is independent evidence the rate is right.

will be rejected and not forwarded for evaluation"* (Q&A #5). At least
50% of the amount paid must go to PRG's own employees or to
similarly-situated (SDVOSB/VOSB) concerns. **A non-SDVOSB 1099 monitor
does not count toward PRG's half.** This price is built on **W2
employees**, which satisfies the certificate cleanly. See §7 — the
