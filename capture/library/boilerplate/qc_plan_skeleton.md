# Quality Control Plan — skeleton

**Dual use.** Tailor this for (a) the *Draft QA Plan* many solicitations
require in the technical volume, and (b) the *live QC plan* you operate
under after award. Same document, different maturity — which is the
point: what you promised is what you run.

Tailoring rule: every control below must trace to a PWS requirement or a
QASP threshold. Delete anything that traces to neither. Generic quality
boilerplate reads as filler and costs evaluation points.

---

## 1. Purpose and scope
One paragraph: the contract, the deliverables covered, and the standards
that govern (cite the PWS/QASP paragraph numbers).

## 2. Quality organization
- **Who holds QC authority** and their independence from production —
  name the role, not just the person.
- **Nothing reaches the customer un-reviewed.** State who performs the
  review and that it happens before release.
- Escalation path when a deficiency is found.

## 3. Performance standards matrix
The core of the plan. One row per PWS/QASP requirement.

| # | Requirement (PWS ¶) | Standard / AQL | How measured | Frequency | Who | Record |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | | |

Pull the standards from the QASP verbatim where one exists. Where the
solicitation states an acceptable quality level, use **their** number —
never invent a tighter one you then have to meet.

## 4. Inspection and surveillance method
For each deliverable class: 100% inspection, or sampling with the plan
stated. Say which and why.

## 5. Deficiency identification and correction
- How a deficiency is detected, recorded, and classified by severity
- Correction timeline by severity
- **Root-cause analysis** for anything recurring, not just a re-do
- Corrective and preventive action (CAPA) tracking to closure
- Customer notification rule: what gets reported, to whom, how fast

## 6. Records
What is retained, where, for how long, and who can produce it on demand.
A QC plan you cannot evidence is not a QC plan.

## 7. Customer feedback and continuous improvement
- COR communication cadence
- How feedback and CPARS input feed back into the standards above
- Periodic plan review and update trigger

## 8. Personnel qualification control
- Credential verification before assignment
- **Currency tracking with advance expiry alerts** — no one performs on
  lapsed credentials
- Training records and refresher cadence

## 9. Subcontractor quality
How flow-downs are imposed and verified, and how sub work is inspected
before it becomes a PRG deliverable. PRG's name is on it either way.
