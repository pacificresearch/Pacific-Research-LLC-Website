## 1. Payment portals (do now — both free, ~1 hr total)

### ⬜ DoD: PIEE / WAWF (pays the Fort Detrick contract)
1. Go to **piee.eb.mil** → "Register" → New User → Vendor.
2. Create your user account (needs company CAGE **1Z9B6**, UEI
   J585TLDV1CH1, your info as admin).
3. Request roles: **"WAWF – Vendor"** (invoicing) for your CAGE.
4. Activation requires the company's **Electronic Business POC** listed
   in SAM to approve — that should be YOU; verify in SAM (below).
5. Done when: you can log into PIEE and see WAWF vendor role active.

### ⬜ VA: e-invoicing via Tungsten Network / VA-FSC (pays the GCP contract)
VA mandates electronic invoices (VAAR 852.232-72) through its
e-invoicing partner (Tungsten Network) to the VA Financial Services
Center.
1. Register a vendor account at Tungsten's portal (tungsten-network.com
   / Kofax invoicing portal), connecting to buyer **Department of
   Veterans Affairs — Financial Services Center**. Free "Web Form"
   tier is sufficient at our volume.
2. Have ready: UEI, TIN/EIN, remit banking (must match SAM EFT).
3. Full vendorization into VA's payment file happens at award (VA Form
   10091 with the CO) — but the portal account existing NOW removes the
   long pole.
4. Done when: Tungsten account active with VA-FSC linked as customer.
   (Verify at award the CO confirms Tungsten vs. any newer VA portal —
   platform names drift; the contract's payment clause governs.)

- ⬜ **Working-capital float**: confirm ~$15K accessible for Fort
  Detrick's first 2 payroll months (pay employee before VA/DFAS net-30
  catches up). GCP contract needs ~$0 float (founder-performed).
- ⬜ **Payroll provider** picked & account opened (Gusto/ADP tier that
  handles MD state taxes + SCA fringe line-items). Do NOT run first
  payroll — just be able to onboard a W2 in 48 hrs.

  Liability, Workers' Comp (MD, one tech), Professional/E&O for
  monitoring services. Broker tip: ask for govcon-familiar carriers

- ⬜ **Bookkeeping**: QuickBooks (or similar) with per-contract job
  costing from day one — CLIN-level revenue/cost tracking feeds
  invoices and the recompete pricing history.

## Sequence check
Everything in §1–2 is doable before ANY award with zero downside, and
§1 is the binding constraint: **an award you cannot invoice against is a
working-capital problem you inflicted on yourself.** §3 items cost
nothing to arrange and only bind/spend at award. §5 is already done.

What genuinely CANNOT be pre-done: reading and diffing the actual award,
seeding the calendar from real dates, background investigations (they
need a contract to initiate and are usually the longest pole), badging
and site access, and executing subcontracts.
Result: award notice → same-week performance start, invoices flowing
the first eligible day.
