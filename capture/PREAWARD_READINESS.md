## 1. Payment portals (do now — both free, ~1 hr total)

### ⬜ DoD: PIEE / WAWF (pays the Fort Detrick contract)
1. Go to **piee.eb.mil** → "Register" → New User → Vendor.
2. Create your user account (needs company CAGE **1Z9B6**, UEI
   J585TLDV1CH1, your info as admin).
3. Request roles: **"WAWF – Vendor"** (invoicing) for your CAGE.
4. Activation requires the company's **Electronic Business POC** listed
   in SAM to approve — that should be YOU; verify in SAM (below).
5. Done when: you can log into PIEE and see WAWF vendor role active.

### ⬜ VA: e-invoicing via Tungsten Network / VA-FSC (pays the GCP contract)
VA mandates electronic invoices (VAAR 852.232-72) through its
e-invoicing partner (Tungsten Network) to the VA Financial Services
Center.
1. Register a vendor account at Tungsten's portal (tungsten-network.com
   / Kofax invoicing portal), connecting to buyer **Department of
   Veterans Affairs — Financial Services Center**. Free "Web Form"
   tier is sufficient at our volume.
2. Have ready: UEI, TIN/EIN, remit banking (must match SAM EFT).
3. Full vendorization into VA's payment file happens at award (VA Form
   10091 with the CO) — but the portal account existing NOW removes the
   long pole.
4. Done when: Tungsten account active with VA-FSC linked as customer.
   (Verify at award the CO confirms Tungsten vs. any newer VA portal —
   platform names drift; the contract's payment clause governs.)
