International Programs

U.S. prime accountability, delivered anywhere.

Pacific Research Group LLC supports U.S. missions, federal agencies operating overseas, and international organizations with the same model that anchors our federal work at home: an accountable U.S. prime contractor, leadership on the ground, and vetted local experts executing to documented quality standards.

Scope of Services

Overseas contract support.

Embassy & Mission Support

Administrative, operations, facilities, and program support services for U.S. embassies, consulates, and missions — delivered through locally sourced, individually vetted professionals under PRG's program management, quality control, and reporting.

Field Research & Program Operations

Research operations, data collection and management, monitoring and reporting for internationally sited programs — grounded in PRG's multi-site federal research operations practice and formal international-studies training.

Local Expert Networks

PRG identifies, vets, contracts, and manages in-country subject-matter experts and firms — compliance-screened, reference-checked, and managed against defined deliverables — giving customers local knowledge without sacrificing a single accountable point of contact.

Logistics & Contingency Operations

Operational support for time-critical, interagency environments. PRG's leadership served in large-scale federal contingency operations, including medical and administrative operations supporting 25,000+ evacuees under interagency and CDC parameters during Operation Allies Welcome.

Delivery Model

On the ground, on the record.

Leadership at post

PRG's Managing Director — a U.S. military veteran with a Master's in International Studies and field experience in South America and Asia — travels to the place of performance to stand up each engagement: sourcing local experts in person, establishing quality standards, and maintaining the customer relationship directly.

Compliance built in

Anti-corruption (FCPA) vetting of every local partner and hire, Defense Base Act coverage where required, export-control screening, and host-country labor-law compliance are standing requirements of PRG's international workflow — not afterthoughts.

One accountable prime

Contract management, quality control, invoicing, and reporting stay with PRG as the U.S.-registered prime (SAM.gov registered; UEI J585TLDV1CH1). Customers get local execution with stateside accountability, in English, on U.S. terms.

Ready for hard places

PRG evaluates and prices high-threat and austere environments deliberately — security, life support, medical coverage, and duty of care for every member of the team — drawing on leadership experience in emergency medicine and austere operations.
