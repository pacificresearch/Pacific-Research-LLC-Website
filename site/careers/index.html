Senior Biomedical Equipment Technician (BMET) — Medical Research Equipment Support

Contingent Upon Contract Award Fort Detrick — Frederick, MD (100% on-site) Full-time, W2 $80,000–$90,000 DOE Anticipated start: September 2026

Pacific Research Group is seeking a Senior BMET to support the Naval Medical Research Command (NMRC) Biological Defense Research Directorate at Fort Detrick, MD. You will be the dedicated contractor BMET for a military medical research laboratory — maintaining research and medical equipment, running the scheduled maintenance program, and managing equipment inventory and accountability in DMLSS. Single-incumbent role with high visibility: your work closes a documented maintenance backlog and keeps a Navy research mission running.

  • Level II scheduled maintenance (inspection, PM, calibration, scheduled parts replacement) on medical and research equipment, documented in DMLSS
  • Equipment inventory and accountability per NAVMED P-5132 and BUMED logistics guidance; annual inventory and monthly GE-R reporting
  • Acceptance inspection of new equipment, warranty/service-contract tracking, operator training, and DRMO/DLA disposition coordination

Required: Military BMET training certificate, manufacturer's training certificate, or AAMI CBET; experience with the military medical research enterprise; DMLSS (or equivalent) working knowledge; U.S. citizenship with ability to obtain SECRET eligibility (Tier 3) — active clearance not required to apply; ability to pass laboratory occupational health screenings. Mon–Fri, 8 hrs/day on-site.

What we offer: $80,000–$90,000 DOE · federal holidays off · stable weekday schedule (no nights, no call) · multi-year engagement (base + option years) · direct access to company leadership — supported by management with hands-on military BMET and AAMI CBET experience.
