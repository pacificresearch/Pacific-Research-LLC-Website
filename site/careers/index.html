Senior Biomedical Equipment Technician (BMET)

Contingent Upon Contract Award Federal medical facilities — site assigned at award Full-time $38–$52/hr DOE

Pacific Research Group LLC, a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, is recruiting a Senior BMET to lead biomedical equipment maintenance and management in support of anticipated federal healthcare contracts (VA/DoD medical facilities). You'll own the preventive-maintenance program, corrective repairs, and accreditation readiness for a facility-scale medical equipment inventory.

  • Perform and document scheduled/corrective maintenance across imaging, lab, and patient-care equipment to manufacturer and Joint Commission / DoD accreditation standards
  • Manage equipment inventory, service records, and vendor coordination in a CMMS; drive ≥95% PM completion
  • Mentor junior BMETs and serve as the on-site technical lead and customer point of contact

Required: 5+ years BMET experience (military 4A2X5/68A or civilian equivalent); AAMI CBET certification or eligibility; experience in accredited hospital environments. U.S. citizenship and ability to pass a federal background investigation.

Apply by Email Attach resume + certifications. Posted August 14, 2026.
