## About Pacific Research Group LLC

Pacific Research Group LLC is a certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) providing biomedical, clinical research, and healthcare technology management support services to federal customers. Our leadership brings hands-on military BMET and AAMI CBET-certified experience — you'll be supported by management that has done this job.

## Position Summary

Pacific Research Group is seeking a **Senior Biomedical and Equipment Inventory/Accountability Technician (BMET)** to support the Naval Medical Research Command (NMRC) Biological Defense Research Directorate at Fort Detrick, MD. You will be the dedicated contractor BMET supporting a military medical research laboratory — maintaining research and medical equipment, running the scheduled maintenance program, and managing equipment inventory and accountability in DMLSS.

This is a single-incumbent role with high visibility and direct impact: your work closes a documented maintenance backlog and keeps a Navy research mission running.

## Key Responsibilities

- Perform Level II scheduled maintenance (inspection, preventive maintenance, calibration, scheduled parts replacement) on medical and research equipment, and document all work in DMLSS
- Perform electrical safety inspections to verify equipment performance and end-user safety
- Maintain equipment inventory and accountability records per NAVMED P-5132 and current BUMED logistics guidance; support the annual equipment inventory and monthly GE-R reporting
- Inspect and accept newly acquired medical/dental/research equipment against safety standards, manufacturer specifications, and contract requirements
- Receive, unpack, insure, log, and deliver inventoried equipment; inspect shipments for damage and document discrepancies
- Monitor manufacturer/vendor warranties and maintenance agreements in DMLSS; enter service contract data (maintenance history, contractor, contract type, cost, contract number, period)
- Review purchase packages for new or replacement equipment and provide technical feedback
- Train equipment operators when new makes or models arrive on site
- Tag equipment for storage or disposition and coordinate DRMO/DLA disposition with property custodians and the Accountable Property Officer
- Identify and report unlocatable equipment; produce the monthly Unable-to-Locate Equipment Report
- Contribute to a monthly progress report covering accomplishments, issues, government-furnished property, and upcoming work plans

- Experience interfacing with the military medical research enterprise (military treatment facilities, DoD research labs, or military medical logistics)
