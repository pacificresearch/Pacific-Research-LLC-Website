## 13. GLOBAL RULE: SDVOSB wording

Use **SDVOSB** in caps everywhere the credential appears in body copy, nav, cards, credential bars, and headings. Do not spell out the phrase in any of those places.

Spell it out in exactly one location, the footer fine print in section 12, and set it lowercase (sentence case) in small muted type. Search the source for "Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business" and confirm only that one instance remains.

Two places worth checking beyond the homepage:
- The Careers page and talent pool copy
- Your LinkedIn company page About section, which currently leads with the full phrase

The acronym carries the same weight with a contracting officer, since it is the term used in FAR 19.14 and in SAM. Nothing is lost by not spelling it out.
